In this work, we use the Robust Scaler method, which works by subtracting the median (med(X)) of the data (X) and scaling in the interval between the 1st (Q1) and the 3rd (Q3) quartiles. The Robust Scaler equation is given by RS(xi)= xi − med(X) Q3 − Q1 .
